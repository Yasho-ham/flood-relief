URGENT FLOOD RELIEF

Help Rebuild Our Home

Our home in Haijalpur, Bihar, was completely destroyed by the recent flooding. We are raising financial support for immediate family needs and recovery.

Who I Am

My name is Satyam Kumar. I am an alumnus of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Saran (2015–2022) and a Dakshana Scholar (BLR, 2020–2022). I later pursued my B.Tech. in Electronics and Communication Engineering at IIT Roorkee (2022–2026).

I never imagined that I would one day have to ask people for help to rebuild the place I called home.

A sudden dam breakage caused an enormous surge of water to enter our village. In a matter of moments, nearly 20 feet of water swept through the area. Our house was completely overwhelmed and swept away along with everything inside it—our belongings, documents, furniture, clothes, and the things our family had built and preserved over many years.

My family was fortunate to somehow survive this disaster, but we have lost our home and almost everything we owned.

The support and opportunities I received from JNV Saran, Dakshana Foundation, and IIT Roorkee have shaped my life in ways I will always be grateful for. Today, I am reaching out to my teachers, friends, colleagues, alumni and well-wishers during one of the most difficult moments my family has ever faced.

I am trying to raise funds for immediate necessities and to rebuild a safe home for my family. Every contribution, no matter how small, will help us take one step forward. And even sharing this appeal with someone who may be able to help would mean a lot to us.
