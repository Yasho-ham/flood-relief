Why bank transfer is preferred

HDFC currently states that incoming UPI Person-to-Person (P2P) payments can have up to 50 successful inward transactions in a rolling 24-hour period. For incoming P2P payments above ₹2,000, HDFC states an additional limit of 25 transactions OR ₹4 lakh total in 24 hours, whichever is reached first. These limits apply at the account level.

Normal UPI P2P payments also generally have a ₹1 lakh per-transaction ceiling, although the donor's own bank or UPI app may impose a lower limit.

For this ₹5–20 lakh fundraiser, please use NEFT / IMPS / RTGS whenever practical.
